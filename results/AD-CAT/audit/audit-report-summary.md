# Audit report summary

|  | App A (Wysa) | App B (MindDoc) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actors | Soukaina Moumou, auditor | Soukaina Moumou, auditor |
| Application information | Wysa: Mental Wellbeing AI, version 7.0.1, iOS 26 and Android, Premium tier (monthly) | MindDoc: Mental Health Support, version 4.18.32, iOS 26 and Android, MindDoc+ tier (3-month plan) |
| Audit summary | Wysa met 22 of 30 applicable items. Coverage was highest for interaction capability, security and privacy, and safety (83.3% each), followed by AI (75.0%), and lowest for functional suitability (50.0%). Its strengths are crisis support (a persistent crisis button with tap-to-call, a personalised safety plan, and crisis resources shown when risk language is detected), disclosure of the AI at first use, and published documentation of the AI's function, limitations and safeguards. | MindDoc met 19 of 26 applicable items; the four AI items were not applicable. Coverage was highest for interaction capability and security and privacy (83.3% each), followed by functional suitability (75.0%), and lowest for safety (50.0%). Its strengths are CBT and ACT tools, validated screening, clinician-shareable export, a published randomised controlled trial, authentication by default, and a privacy policy that names GDPR and the data storage location. |
| Coverage score | 73.3% (22 of 30 items) | 73.1% (19 of 26 items) |
| Deficiencies | No peer support and no in-app export of data to a clinician. Screening is not clearly based on a validated instrument. Access to licensed professionals requires an additional paid subscription. Authentication is optional rather than required by default. Users can only partly intervene in the AI feature. Accessibility support is partial. | Suicidality is assessed, but no crisis resources are shown with the result. There is no personalised safety plan. Professional support is available only through an external service with an additional subscription. Account and data deletion within the app is incomplete. Accessibility support is partial, and there is no peer support. |
| Recommendations | Add in-app export of mood history and reports for sharing with clinicians. Base self-assessment on named validated instruments such as PHQ-9 and GAD-7. Require a passcode or biometric lock by default. Let users stop the AI, reject its suggestions or switch to a non-AI tool at any point. Improve support for system text size and screen readers. | Show crisis contacts immediately whenever the assessment indicates suicide risk. Add a personalised safety plan. Allow irreversible deletion of the account and all data from within the app. Improve support for system text size and screen readers. |
