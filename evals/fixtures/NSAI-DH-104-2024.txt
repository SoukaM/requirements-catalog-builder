NSAI/DH-104:2024
National Standard for Digital Health Applications Supporting Adult Mental Wellbeing
Part 2: Product Requirements
(excerpt: Clauses 5 to 7)

5. Data handling

5.1 Retention
5.1.1 A digital health application shall retain personally identifiable user-entered
      wellbeing records for no longer than 24 months after the user's last active
      session, unless the user has explicitly opted in to longer retention.
5.1.2 Where a user requests deletion of their account, the application shall erase
      all personally identifiable wellbeing records within 30 days of the request.
5.1.3 Aggregate statistics derived from user records may be retained indefinitely
      provided they cannot be re-identified.

5.2 Portability
5.2.1 The application shall enable the user to export their complete wellbeing
      record in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
5.2.2 NOTE An export offered only as a rendered PDF does not satisfy 5.2.1.

5.3 Transmission
5.3.1 All personally identifiable data transmitted between the application and any
      remote service shall be protected in transit using a current, non-deprecated
      transport encryption protocol.

6. Safety

6.1 Risk signals
6.1.1 Where an application collects free-text entries from the user, it shall
      provide the user with a persistently reachable route to crisis support
      information from any screen on which free-text entry is possible.
6.1.2 The application shall not present the user with an automated assessment of
      suicide risk.
6.1.3 The application shall display, on first use, a statement that it is not a
      substitute for professional care.

6.2 Content provenance
6.2.1 Any therapeutic content presented to the user shall carry an identifiable
      source and a date of last clinical review.
6.2.2 Therapeutic content whose last clinical review is more than 36 months old
      shall be withdrawn or re-reviewed.

7. User interaction

7.1 Accessibility
7.1.1 The application shall conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.
7.1.2 The application shall permit the user to increase text size to at least 200 %
      of the default without loss of content or functionality.

7.2 Notifications
7.2.1 The application shall permit the user to disable all non-essential
      notifications, and shall retain that preference across application updates.
7.2.2 The application shall not send engagement-driven notifications between
      22:00 and 07:00 in the user's local time zone unless the user has explicitly
      configured otherwise.

7.3 Personalisation
7.3.1 Where the application adapts content on the basis of user data, it shall
      disclose to the user that adaptation is occurring and on what categories of
      data it is based.

Terms and definitions
3.4 wellbeing record: structured or free-text entry created by a user describing
    their mental state, mood, activity or symptoms.
